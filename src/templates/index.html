How SmartFlow AI reaches a prediction

Three groups of inputs feed one trained model that returns a congestion category.

Traffic Intelligence

Vehicle count, average speed, traffic density and queue length describe how busy a road is right now. The model compares these measurements with the road type, lane count and speed limit to judge how close the road is to its capacity.

Environmental Context

Weather condition, hour of day and weekend status add context to the traffic measurements. The same number of vehicles can lead to a different congestion level in clear weather than in heavy rain or at rush hour.

AI-Powered Analysis

The dashboard sends your inputs to a trained machine learning model through a FastAPI backend. The model returns one congestion category: Low, Moderate, High or Severe.

How it works

  1. Enter road and traffic details.
  2. Submit the information to the FastAPI backend.
  3. The preprocessing pipeline transforms the inputs.
  4. The trained ML model returns a congestion category.
  5. The dashboard displays the result.
